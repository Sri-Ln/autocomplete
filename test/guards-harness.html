Guards harness

Loads the real content/guards.js and content/filler.js. The rule under test: a field that is not on the page is not the same as a field sitting there empty. Tenants render different subsets of the same form — the Create Account terms checkbox is absent on some of them — and refusing to submit over a field that does not exist leaves the user nothing to fix. Absent falls through to unknownRequiredEmpty(), which reads the DOM rather than our selector map, so a rotted selector over a genuinely required field is still caught.
