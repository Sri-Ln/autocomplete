Flow harness — the orchestrator in content/main.js

Loads every content script in manifest order against a React-controlled Workday fixture, with chrome stubbed, and then drives the real widget button inside its shadow root with a real click. Nothing here calls doFill or doSubmit directly — those are private to main.js, and the whole point is to exercise the path a user takes: click → unlock check → fill → guards → submit.

Three fixtures, one at a time. Only one may be mounted, because AF.currentPage returns the FIRST page whose detector matches and Create Account is declared before My Information — both on screen and My Information would never be reached. The third, the "we emailed you a link" screen, is one the registry recognises as nothing at all; the checks numbered 8 are about what the widget offers there, and about the link it shows never being followed for you.
